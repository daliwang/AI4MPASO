The restart contract

Copy year-55 restart. Overwrite only deep T and S (k = 46…60). Keep thickness, velocity, mesh, xtime.

60 month-aligned pairs, Δ = 550 yr. Train years 51–54 (48). Hold out 55 → 605 (12).

v1 does not predict SSH, velocity, or the upper ocean.
